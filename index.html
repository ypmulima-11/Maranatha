Welcome to Maranatha

So much more than just a choir

A spirit-filled choir dedicated to enhancing worship and serving the community through music.

Maranatha Choir is a dedicated choir based in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. Founded on the belief that music is a powerful form of prayer, we strive to lead our congregation into deeper worship and encounter with God.

Our members come from diverse backgrounds — all united by faith and a deep love of sacred music. We minister at Sunday services, feast days, special celebrations, and community events throughout the year.
