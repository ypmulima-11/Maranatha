This portal needs a free Supabase project to store accounts and resources. To finish the setup:

  1. Open supabase-config.js in the site files — the full step-by-step instructions are at the top of that file.
  2. Paste your project URL and anon key into it.
  3. Run supabase-setup.sql once in the Supabase SQL editor.
  4. Commit and push — the login below switches on automatically.
